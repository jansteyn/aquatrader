These data fields belong to a Sharenet stock price export layout, which is widely used to track financial metrics and daily market activity on African exchanges like the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE).
Here is what each column header means, grouped by category:
🏢 Identification & Classification
• DN_NAME: Data Vendor Name. This is the official abbreviated short name of the stock assigned by Sharenet.
• FULL_NAME: The full, registered legal name of the company.
• CODE: The ticker symbol or alphanumeric code used to trade the stock on the exchange (e.g., BIL for BHP Group).
• ISIN: International Securities Identification Number. A unique 12-character global code used to flawlessly identify the specific security.
• EXCHANGE: The code representing the financial marketplace where the asset is traded (e.g., JSE for equity, SAFEX for futures).
• DNSECTOR: The proprietary industry sector classification code assigned by Sharenet.
• EXCHANGE_SECTOR: The official industry classification assigned directly by the stock exchange.
• AMI_SECTOR: African Market Index Sector classification, used for tracking and benchmarking regional index groupings.
📈 Daily Price Data
• DATE: The trading day for the recorded data, formatted as YYYYMMDD.
• OPEN: The opening market price of the stock when the trading session began for the day.
• HIGH: The highest price the stock reached during that specific trading session.
• LOW: The lowest price the stock dropped to during that specific trading session.
• CLOSE: The final price at which the stock traded when the market closed for the day.
• BID: The highest price a buyer is currently willing to pay for the stock at the end of the day.
• OFFER: The lowest price a seller is currently willing to accept for the stock at the end of the day (also called the "Ask").
• CURRENCY: The currency denomination in which the stock prices are quoted (e.g., ZAR for South African Rand, USD for US Dollars).
📊 Volume & Activity Metrics
• VOLUME: The total number of individual shares bought and sold throughout the day.
• VALUE: The total monetary value of all the shares traded during the day (Volume × Price).
• DEALS: The total number of completed transactions (trades) executed during the day.
• OI (Open Interest): The total number of outstanding derivative contracts (like options or futures) that have not been settled or closed out. (Note: This will usually be 0 for standard equities and only active for futures).
💡 Valuation & Structural Data
• DY (Dividend Yield): The company's annual dividend payment divided by its current share price, expressed as a percentage. It shows your cash-flow return on investment.
• EY (Earnings Yield): The company's earnings per share (EPS) divided by its current share price, expressed as a percentage. It is the inverse of the P/E ratio and shows how profitable a company is relative to its stock price.
• ISSUED_SHARES: The total number of authorized shares that the company has created and distributed to shareholders (outstanding shares).
